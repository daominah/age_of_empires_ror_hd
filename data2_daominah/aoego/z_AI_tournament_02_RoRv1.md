## Round of 16

### Ro16. Pair1. Roman vs Hittite

#### Game 1

Roman strategies: Rome_Siege x2, Rome_Legion x1

Hittite strategies: Hittite_Horse_Archer x2, Hittite_Catapult x1

Result:

- Hittite using Heavy Horse Archer is disadvantaged against Roman Siege.
  if they had Heavy Catapult, they would have a huge edge.
- Roman win.

#### Game 2

Roman strategies: Rome_Siege x3

Hittite strategies: Hittite_Horse_Archer x3

Result:

- Too greedy strategy from Roman in small map.
- Mass Chariot Archer defeat 2 Roman before they can build Siege.
- Hittite win.

#### Game 3

Roman strategies: Rome_Legion x2, Rome_Siege x1

Hittite strategies:

Result:

- All players reachs Iron Age and build their scheduled units.
- A winged Rome is kited by Heavy Horse Archer and defeated.
- But not easy from here, the Hittite with Heavy Catapult did a heavy lifting
  (because Heavy Horse Archer micro is bad, feed to Tower).
- At 1h20m, only 1 Roman can train Legion,
  2 weak Hittite can train Horse Archer, almost no Catapult,
  Legion is much easier to produce,
  in theory Horse Archer can kill infinite Legion, but not in AI control,
- All Hittite build Tower in the wrong side, not facing the Roman.
- At 2h, still timeline 7/3, give Roman the win. Very close game.

### Ro16. Pair2. Carthaginian vs Babylonian

#### Game 1

Carthaginian strategies: Carthage_Camel x3

Babylonian strategies: Babylon_Tower_Priest x3, Babylon_Chariot x1

Result:

- All in Camel from Carthaginian severely damaged 2 Babylonian very soon.
- Later try to use Camel to take down Balista Tower is funny.
- But the surviving Babylonian build Wonder.
- Elephant Archer bad vs Tower, Priest, Catapult.
- Babylonian win when Carthaginian still have timeline advantage.

#### Game 2

Carthaginian strategies: Carthage_Helepolis x3

Babylonian strategies: Babylon_Tower_Priest x3

Result:

- Very unfortunate strategy from Carthage in small map.
- Babylonian Heavy Catapult & Priest completely counter Helepolis & Elephant.
- Babylonian defeat Carthaginian before their Wonder trigger.
