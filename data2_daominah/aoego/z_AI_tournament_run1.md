# Tournament

## Rule

- team 3 vs 3 (for spectator, 1 team has 4 players, but the spectator delete Town Center and move to a corner)
- map type Hill Country, game 1 size Huge, game 2 size Large
- all members in a team have the same civilization
- best of 2. if 1-1, play more, or decide by which team has "nicer" gameplay (e.g. aggressive)

## Round of 16

### Ro16. Pair1. Assyrian vs Babylonian

#### Game 1

Assyrian strategies: Assyria_Archer x3

Babylonian strategies: Babylon_Tower_Priest x3

Result:

- Bronze Assyrian Chariot Archer and Stone Thrower attack Babylonian Towers, feed kills.
- Babylonian get Iron Age Guard Tower very fast. Then Ballista Tower right after.
- Later Horse Archer, Catapult from Assyrian weaker than Babylonian Massive Catapult.
- Babylonian actively build Wonders, Assyrian just throw armies to the Wonders.
- Babylonian win by resolving Wonders in 40 minutes.

#### Game 2

Assyrian strategies: Assyria_Archer x3

Babylonian strategies: Babylon_Chariot x2, Babylon_Tower_Priest x1

Result:

- Middle Babylonian at the corner go for Tower strategy, would be better if he is a wing.
- Does not matter, Assyrians feed some Chariot Archer to Towers,
  then fully upgraded Scythe Chariot with 1 pierce armor from Babylonian comes out too strong.
- Winged Assyrians are obliterated in 15 minutes.
- The last Assyrian with Alchemy Horse Archer got a lot of kills, but 1 vs 3 is hard.
- Babylonian win. Overall 2-0.

### Ro16. Pair2. Carthaginian vs Choson

#### Game 1

Carthaginian strategies: Carthage_Helepolis x3

Choson strategies: Choson_Tower x2, Choson_Swordsmen x1

Result:

- Choson can mess up their Swordsman train timing.
- Carthage early mass Camel quickly kill 1 Choson.  
  But if Swordsman come out normally, Choson can defend well. Priest heal too.
- Choson power spike with Longswordman kill 2 Carthage in 18 minutes.
- Later Helepolis is strong vs Swordsman, but Choson has a lot of Priest.
- Choson win by dominating the timeline.
- There is a game 0 where all Choson bug Swordsman, record 1 win for Carthage too.

#### Game 2

Carthaginian strategies: Carthage_Helepolis x3

Choson strategies: Choson_Swordsmen x3

Result:

- Carthage don't attack with Camel early enough.
- Still 1 Choson mess up Swordsman train timing.
- The Longswordman power spike obliterates winged Carthage again.
- Choson win.
