# Tournament

## Rule

- Team 3 vs 3 (for spectator, 1 team has 4 players, but the spectator delete Town Center and move to a corner)
- Map type Hill Country, map size Huge Large Huge for game 1, 2, 3 respectively
- All members in a team have the same civilization
- Best of 3

## Round of 16

### Ro16. Pair1. Assyrian vs Babylonian

#### Game 1

Assyrian strategies: Assyria_Archer x3

Babylonian strategies: Babylon_Tower_Priest x3

Result:

- Bronze Assyrian Chariot Archer and Stone Thrower attack Babylonian Towers, feed kills.
- Babylonian get Iron Age Guard Tower very fast. Then Ballista Tower right after.
- Later Horse Archer, Catapult from Assyrian weaker than Babylonian Massive Catapult.
- Babylonian actively build Wonders, Assyrian just throw armies to the Wonders.
- Babylonian win by resolving Wonders in 40 minutes.

#### Game 2

Assyrian strategies: Assyria_Archer x3

Babylonian strategies: Babylon_Chariot x2, Babylon_Tower_Priest x1

Result:

- Middle Babylonian at the corner go for Tower strategy, would be better if he is a wing.
- Does not matter, Assyrians feed some Chariot Archer to Towers,
  then fully upgraded Scythe Chariot with 1 pierce armor from Babylonian comes out too strong.
- Winged Assyrians are obliterated in 15 minutes.
- The last Assyrian with Alchemy Horse Archer got a lot of kills, but 1 vs 3 is hard.
- Babylonian win. Overall 2-0.

### Ro16. Pair2. Carthaginian vs Choson

#### Game 1

Carthaginian strategies: Carthage_Helepolis x3

Choson strategies: Choson_Tower x2, Choson_Swordsmen x1

Result:

- Choson can mess up their Swordsman train timing.
- Carthage early mass Camel quickly kill 1 Choson.  
  But if Swordsman come out normally, Choson can defend well. Priest heal too.
- Choson power spike with Longswordman kill 2 Carthage in 18 minutes.
- Later Helepolis is strong vs Swordsman, but Choson has a lot of Priest.
- Choson win by dominating the timeline.
- There is a game 0 where all Choson bug Swordsman, record 1 win for Carthage too.

#### Game 2

Carthaginian strategies: Carthage_Helepolis x3

Choson strategies: Choson_Swordsmen x3

Result:

- Carthage don't attack with Camel early enough.
- Still 1 Choson mess up Swordsman train timing.
- The Longswordman power spike obliterates winged Carthage again.
- Choson win.

### Ro16. Pair3. Egyptian vs Greek

#### Game 1

Egyptian strategies: Egypt_Chariot_Priest x3

Greek strategies: Greek_Centurion x3

Result:

- A winged Greek is obliterated at 12 minutes by Camel and Chariot. Defeated at 20m.
  Hoplite is not fast enough. Priest join the fight soon.
- Centurion gets a lot of kills, can take down Tower too.
- In the long run, Egyptian have much more Villager and Priest convert a lot.
- Egyptian win by dominating the timeline (Greek has good Tower).

#### Game 2

Egyptian and Greek currently only have 1 strategy.

Result:

- Hoplite arrived in time to fight Camel and Chariot,
  attack before Egyptian have enough Priest,
  a winged Egyptian is destroyed, balance on the other wing.
- Phalanx defeat the middle Egyptian too, they are quite strong in small map.
- Greek win by destroying everything in 19 minutes.

#### Game 3

Result:

- Chariot Archer and Priest beat a winged Greek.
- The middle Greek has 3 small group of Centurion at 20 minutes, quite strong.
- Egypt simply grind out the Greek. Egyptian win.

### Ro16. Pair4. Hittite vs Macedonian

#### Game 1

Hittite strategies: Hittite_Horse_Archer x2, Hittite_Catapult x1

Macedonian strategies: Macedon_Centurion x3

Result:

- Even map Huge, Hittite Chariot Archer is a counter of +2 pierce Mace Hoplite,
  they still hit fast enough to dominate the timeline at 15 minutes.
- The last Hittite with Heavy Horse Archer & Catapult kill many Centurion & Ballista.
- Macedonian win by dominating the timeline. Hittite could grind if not 1 vs 3.

#### Game 2

Hittite strategies: Hittite_Horse_Archer x2, Hittite_Catapult x1

Macedonian strategies: Macedon_Centurion x3

Result:

- Hittite early mass Chariot Archer and some Stone Thrower defeat 1 Macedonian.
- Macedonian manage to severely damage a Hittite, it turns into a 2 vs 2.
- Later both Heavy Horse Archer or Heavy Catapult can easily fight Centurion.
- Hittite win.

#### Game 3

Hittite strategies: Hittite_Horse_Archer x2, Hittite_Catapult x1

Macedonian strategies: Macedon_Centurion x3

Result:

- Hoplite and a few Cavalry attack before Chariot Archer mass, defeat a Hittite.
- Hittite armies absolutely have better quality.
- But Ballista can fight Heavy Horse Archer after Hittite throw some into Tower.
- Macedonian win by having timeline advantage. Very unexpected outcome.

### Ro16. Pair5. Minoan vs Palmyran

#### Game 1

Minoan strategies: Minoa_Bowmen_Helepolis x3

Palmyran strategies: Palmyra_Camel x3

Result:

- Minoan Composite Bowmen and some early Hoplite resist Camel a little bit,
  still 1 Minoan is defeated early.
- Later, Composite Bowmen and Helepolis fight Scythe Chariot and a few Horse Archer.
- Minoan armies have better quality, especially Palmyran don't have good Catapult.
- Timeline is 50/50 from 30 minutes, 1 Palmyran has nothing left, 2 vs 2.
- Minoan clearly win at 45 minutes.

#### Game 2

Minoan strategies: Minoa_Bowmen_Helepolis x3

Palmyran strategies: Palmyra_Camel x2, Palmyra_Horse_Archer x1

Result:

- Palmyran too strong in small map.
- Palmyran win in 13 minutes, fastest game in the tournament.

#### Game 3

Minoan strategies: Minoa_Bowmen_Helepolis x3

Palmyran strategies: Palmyra_Camel x2, Palmyra_Horse_Archer x1

Result:

- Early Bronze, Minoan Sentry Tower and Hoplite hold, but still damaged.
- At 15 minutes, Palmyran has Scythe Chariot, group with Camel,
  2 Minoan are defeated after reaching Iron Age not too long.
- The Palmyran that plays Heavy Horse Archer also plays Catapult.
- Palmyran win.

### Ro16. Pair6. Persian vs Phoenician

#### Game 1

Persian strategies: Persia_War_Elephant x3

Phoenician strategies: Phoenicia_Elephant_Archer x3

Result:

- Early Bronze, mass Chariot Archer already have advantage over a few Camel.
- Later Priest just crush War/Armored Elephant.
- Persian Catapult and Priest resist Elephant Archer a little bit.
- Phoenician clearly win.
  They have a lot of Villager. Elephant Archer stay under Tower to take them down.

#### Game 2

Persian and Phoenician currently only have 1 strategy.

Result:

- Tower and Camel actually hold in the Bronze Age.
- Very soon later, Persian reach Iron Age, push with War Elephant, Catapult, Priest.
- At 30 minutes, 2 winged Phoenician are severely damaged.
- But it is very hard to train Elephant, then feed to Priest.
- Eventually Phoenician rebuild and win.

### Ro16. Pair7. Roman vs Shang

#### Game 1

Roman strategies: Rome_Siege x3

Shang strategies: Shang_Cataphract x3

Result:

- Mass Cavalry attack too fast, at 20 minutes, 2 Roman are defeated.
- Without Priest from Roman, Cataphract show their strength, tough and fast,
  slowly take down very strong Roman Siege forces.
- Shang win.

#### Game 2

Roman strategies: Rome_Legion x2, Rome_Siege x1

Shang strategies: Shang_Stable x2, Shang_Cataphract x1

Result:

- Roman Swordsman is so strong,
  can fight mass Cavalry in Bronze Age,
  have advantage in Iron Age too.
- But Cataphract and Shang have better economy win.

### Ro16. Pair8. Sumerian vs Yamato

#### Game 1

Sumerian strategies: Sumeria_Catapult x3

Yamato strategies: Yamato_Cavalry x3

Result:

- Super mass Cavalry from Yamato in Bronze so hard to stop.
- 15' a Sumerian is defeated. 20' another Sumerian is defeated.
- Even in the Iron Age, Heavy Catapult and Horse Archer are not good
  vs Cataphract, and Yamato use Horse Archer too to prevent kiting.
- Unlucky match up. Yamato absolutely win.

#### Game 2

Sumerian and Yamato currently only have 1 strategy.

Result:

- In smaller map, Yamato Cavalry even stronger.
- At 12 minutes, 2 Sumerian are almost defeated.
- Yamato win.
