Strong vs archers and Spearmen, weak vs melee units and siege units.

## Technologies

- Attack:
  - Fletching +1, Bodkin Arrow +1, Bracer +1, Chemistry +1.
  - Atlatl +1, Aztecs only.
- Range:
  - Fletching +1, Bodkin Arrow +1, Bracer +1.
  - Atlatl +1, Aztecs only.
  - Yeomen +1, Britons only.
  - Andean Sling eliminates minimum range, Inca only.
  - Chemistry is listed under range on the Skirmisher page only.
- Accuracy: Thumb Ring (100% for non-moving targets), Ballistics (hit moving targets).
- Additional projectiles: Hul'che Javelineers +1, Maya only.
- Armor:
  - Padded Archer Armor +1 melee, +1 pierce.
  - Leather Archer Armor +1 melee, +1 pierce.
  - Ring Archer Armor +1 melee, +2 pierce.
  - Tower Shields +2 pierce, Lithuanians only.
- Conversion resistance: Devotion, Faith, Heresy, First Crusade (Sicilians only).
- Resource cost: Kshatriyas -25% food, Gurjaras only.
- Creation speed: Conscription +33%.
- Resource generation: Tuntian 1.8 food per minute, Wei only.
- Ability: Lamellar Armor, 25% non-projectile damage reflection, Khitans only.
- Other: Malon, 30% pass-through damage, Mapuche only.
- Aura enhancements:
  - Liu Bei +45 hit points per minute, Shu only.
  - Cao Cao +8.75% attack speed, Wei only.
  - Sun Jian +15% movement speed, Wu only.
  - Settlement +15 hit points per minute, Muisca only (Imperial Skirmisher page only).

## Civilization bonuses, per unit

| Bonus                 | Skirmisher                    | Elite Skirmisher        | Imperial Skirmisher |
|-----------------------|-------------------------------|-------------------------|---------------------|
| Mapuche hit points    | +5/+10/+15 Feudal/Castle/Imp. | +10/+15 Castle/Imperial | +15                 |
| Muisca melee armor    | +1/+2/+3 Feudal/Castle/Imp.   | +2/+3 Castle/Imperial   | +3                  |
| Inca food cost        | -20%/-25%/-30% F/C/I          | -25%/-30% Castle/Imp.   | -30%                |
| Mapuche training time | x2.15/x1.61/x1 F/C/I          | x1.61/x1 Castle/Imp.    | not listed          |

Bonuses that are the same for all three units:

- Hit points: Vietnamese +20%.
- Attack: Tatars +20% damage from higher elevation (+50% instead of +25%).
- Damage resistance:
  - Georgians -20% damage, -40% instead of -25% on higher elevation.
  - Jurchens -50% friendly fire damage.
  - Sicilians -40% bonus damage.
- Attack speed: Dravidians +25%.
- Movement speed: Lithuanians +10%.
- Resource cost: Byzantines -25%, Koreans -50% wood,
  Saxons -5% per Town Center or Castle (max -20%) (Skirmisher and Elite Skirmisher pages only).
- Creation speed: Aztecs +15%, Khitans +15%.
- Resource generation: Danes 25% of target cost when destroying buildings
  (not on the Imperial Skirmisher page).
- Upgrading: Chinese -10%/-15% cost in Castle/Imperial Age, Khitans +15% faster
  (Skirmisher page only, for the Elite Skirmisher page it applies to Vietnamese and allies).

Technology bonuses (text of the civilization bonus lists):

- Bohemians: Chemistry is available in the Castle Age (not on the Imperial Skirmisher page).
- Bulgarians: Blacksmith technologies that benefit the unit cost -50% food.
- Burmese: Devotion and Faith are 50% cheaper.
- Celts: can convert herdable animals even if enemy units are next to them.
- Chinese: technologies that benefit the unit are 5%/10%/15% cheaper in Feudal/Castle/Imperial.
- Italians: Ballistics and Chemistry are 25% cheaper.
- Koreans: Padded, Leather and Ring Archer Armor are free.
- Mapuche: can be trained from the Settlement (training time in the table above).
- Shu: Thumb Ring and Blacksmith technologies that benefit the unit are 25% cheaper.
- Spanish: Blacksmith technologies that benefit the unit cost no gold,
  and each such technology provides 20 gold when researched.
- Tatars: Thumb Ring is free.
- Tupi: returns 15% of its cost when killed.
- Turks: Chemistry is free (Skirmisher page only).
- Vietnamese: Conscription is free (not on the Imperial Skirmisher page).

## Team bonuses

- Attack: Saracens +3 vs standard buildings.
- Conversion resistance: Teutons +3 min, +1 max.
- Line of sight: Mapuche +2.
- Creation speed: Britons +10%.
- Upgrading: Britons +10% faster, Portuguese +25% faster.
- Britons: Thumb Ring is researched 10% faster.
- Bulgarians: Blacksmith technologies that benefit the unit are researched 80% faster.
- Lithuanians: Devotion, Faith and Heresy are researched 20% faster.
- Malians: Ballistics and Chemistry are researched 80% faster.
- Portuguese: technologies that benefit the unit are researched 25% faster.
- Vietnamese: the Imperial Skirmisher upgrade is available at the Archery Range in the
  Imperial Age (Elite Skirmisher page).
